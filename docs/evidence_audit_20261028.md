## Corrections made during this audit

1. `paper1_model/make_report.py` now reads the embedding strength from
   `quality_metrics.csv` instead of writing `alpha=24` unconditionally.
2. `paper1_model/make_external_report.py` now evaluates Ours and external
   methods on their common 50-image subset.
3. `paper1_model/results_external_baselines/matched_comparison.csv` records the
   matched comparison in machine-readable form.
4. `paper1_model/results_full_capture_scan/experiment_report.md` was regenerated
   with the correct `alpha=28` configuration.
5. The copy/transplant and flat-image stress tests were rerun at `alpha=28`.
6. A new image-disjoint calibration script produced a 100/50 calibration split
   and a separate 100/50 held-out split, with cluster-bootstrap AUC confidence
   intervals.
7. All 17 ablation configurations were rerun on the full 200/50/50 dataset.
   Carrier, coding, and verification modes use `alpha=28`; the alpha sweep
   covers 8--28.
8. A DCT-only compound-attack run was added. It is stronger than DWT-DCT on
   crop+JPEG and crop+resize+JPEG, while DWT-DCT is substantially stronger on
   blur+JPEG (96.0% versus 29.5%).
9. Two visual-evidence figures were added: a multi-method visibility comparison
   and an end-to-end clean/JPEG/crop/print-scan verification gallery.
10. Fig. 3 now includes the 8 x 8 DCT coefficient map; Fig. 4 separates the
    ORB/flat hard gate from the weighted score; Fig. 5 includes enlarged
    patches and full-sample PSNR/SSIM distributions; Fig. 9 now includes BER.
11. Table 4 no longer duplicates trust-level counts, and Table 7 was split into
    a security table and a calibration/negative/physical-stress table.
12. A dedicated verification-attribution run replayed all 200 final
    `alpha=28` carriers under 21 single/compound conditions and stored all
    payload, registry, content-binding, trust, and alignment fields. Of 4,200
    trajectories, 3,693 were accepted and all 507 rejections first failed
    payload integrity.
13. The complete `results_full_capture_scan` run is now the canonical
    single-attack source. Compared with the earlier partial run, only
    salt-and-pepper acceptance changed, from 198/200 to 199/200; mean BER was
    unchanged. The earlier directory is retained with a `SUPERSEDED.md` marker.

## Figure files not to reuse directly

The images under `paper1_model/results_full/figures/` were generated from an
earlier main run and predate the final `alpha=28` report. Do not copy them into
the manuscript. Regenerate the final versions from the canonical directories
listed in `final_figure_table_plan.md`.
